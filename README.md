# Best AI Room Design Tools (2026)

A curated list of the best AI room design and AI interior design tools in 2026, grouped by the job they do: furnishing an empty room from a photo, redesigning a furnished room, previewing a renovation, professional rendering, and to-scale floor planning. Last checked: October 2026.

AI room design tools take a photo of a room (or a sketch, floor plan, or CAD file), apply a style, and return a photorealistic image of that space redesigned. They differ mostly in what they preserve from the original photo, whether they expect an empty or furnished room, how much you can try for free, and who the output is for.

Tools are sorted alphabetically within each section, not ranked. Descriptions are based on each vendor's own site. Pricing changes often, so it is linked rather than quoted in detail. ## Empty-room furnishing and virtual staging

Tools that add furniture and decor to a photo of an empty (or emptied) room. The real-estate version of this is called virtual staging.

- [AI HomeDesign](https://aihomedesign.com) - Toolbox for property listing photos: virtual staging, photo enhancement, and renovation previews, aimed at agents and photographers.
- [ApplyDesign](https://www.applydesign.io) - Virtual staging with a drag-and-drop editor and per-image pricing, with design replication across multiple photos of the same space.
- [Collov AI](https://collov.ai) - Virtual staging for realtors and brokerages, with multi-angle staging, furniture removal, and listing photo edits ([pricing](https://collov.ai/pricing)).
- [InteriorStage](https://interiorstage.com) - Upload a photo of an empty room, choose a style, and preview movable furniture and decor in that room while walls, floors, windows, and fixtures stay exactly as photographed. Built for homeowners and renters furnishing a new place. Free plan includes 2 watermarked images a month ([pricing](https://interiorstage.com/pricing)).
- [ReimagineHome](https://www.reimaginehome.ai) - Virtual staging, "empty your space", furnished-room redesign, and landscaping from Styldod, with conversational editing on paid plans ([pricing](https://www.reimaginehome.ai/pricing)).
- [Virtual Staging AI](https://www.virtualstagingai.app) - Virtual staging for real estate listings with furniture removal and a per-image credit model.

## Photo-to-redesign apps

General-purpose apps that restyle any room photo, furnished or not, and often repaint walls, replace flooring, or redesign exteriors as well.

- [DecorAI](https://www.decoraihome.com) - Free daily designs with no sign-up, on iOS, Android, and web, covering interiors, exteriors, and gardens.
- [Decoratly](https://www.decoratly.com) - Photo-to-redesign app with a free first redesign, no sign-up, plus furniture removal and color palette tools.
- [GenRoom](https://genroom.io) - Redesigns rooms, exteriors, and backyards from a photo with 60+ styles and 4K upscaling.
- [Homestyler](https://www.homestyler.com) - Free 3D home design software with floor planning, a large 3D model library, cloud rendering, and AI design credits on paid plans ([pricing](https://www.homestyler.com/pricing)).
- [Remodel AI](https://www.remodelai.io) - Interior, exterior, virtual staging, floor, paint, and object-removal tools in one app; 3 free designs with no card ([pricing](https://www.remodelai.io/pricing)).
- [RoomGPT](https://www.roomgpt.io) - One of the earliest photo-to-redesign tools; one free generation, then credit packs instead of a subscription.

## How to choose an AI room design tool

1. **Is the room empty or furnished?** Empty-room tools preserve the architecture and only add furniture. Redesign apps will happily repaint walls and swap floors, which is useful for renovation previews and distracting when you only wanted a sofa.
2. **Are you shopping or renovating?** For furniture decisions, pick a tool that leaves the room structure alone. For paint, flooring, and exteriors, pick a redesign app.
3. **Do you need measurements?** Photo-based tools do not know your room's dimensions. If the question is "will it fit?", use a floor planner, then preview the look in a photo tool.
4. **How much can you try for free?** Free tiers range from one generation to a few designs a day. Spend them on contrasting styles of the same room rather than near-identical variations.
5. **Who is the output for?** Personal shopping decisions, a real estate listing, or a client presentation each point to a different section above.

## FAQ

**What is the best AI for interior design?**
There is no single best tool. For furnishing an empty room from a photo, use a virtual staging tool such as InteriorStage or ReimagineHome. For restyling a furnished room, use a redesign app such as Remodel AI or DecorAI. For client renders from CAD, use Interior AI or Spacely AI. For layouts, use Planner 5D.

**Is there a free AI room design app?**
Yes. DecorAI offers free daily designs with no sign-up, Remodel AI gives 3 free designs, RoomGPT gives one free generation, and InteriorStage includes 2 watermarked images a month on its free plan.

**Can AI design an empty room?**
Yes. Virtual staging tools are built for exactly this: they add movable furniture and decor to a photo of an empty room. Check whether the tool keeps the walls, floors, and windows unchanged, since some redesign apps alter them.

**Do AI interior design tools change walls and floors?**
Some do. Empty-room tools such as InteriorStage keep the room structure as photographed. Redesign apps such as Remodel AI and ReimagineHome include paint, flooring, and renovation modes that change it.
